Informed Consent to Participate in Research

Study Title: Trial Experience and Preparedness Among Criminal Law Attorneys in Tennessee

Institution: Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU)

Faculty Supervisor: Christopher Westmoreland

Purpose of the Study

You are invited to participate in a research study examining trial preparedness among attorneys practicing criminal law in Tennessee. The purpose of this research is to better understand attorneys’ experiences with criminal trials, pretrial motions, and the resources, training, and professional experiences that influence trial preparation.

Eligibility

You must be at least 18 years of age and practice criminal law in Tennessee as a prosecutor or defense attorney to participate. This study is intended for adult legal professionals.

Study Procedures

If you agree to participate, you will complete an online questionnaire administered through Qualtrics. The questionnaire includes questions about your professional background, years of legal practice, jury trial experience, pretrial motions, and perceived trial preparedness.

Voluntary Participation

Your participation is entirely voluntary. You may choose not to participate, skip questions you do not wish to answer, or discontinue the survey at any time without penalty.

Risks and Benefits

The study involves minimal anticipated risk. Some participants may experience minor discomfort when evaluating their professional experiences or preparedness.

There is no expected direct benefit from participating.

Privacy and Confidentiality

The survey is designed to protect participants’ privacy. Participants will not be asked to provide their names in the main questionnaire. Survey responses will be used for research purposes, and access to the data will be limited to authorized members of the research team.

Because the survey collects professional background information and may include open-ended responses, complete anonymity cannot be guaranteed.
